Build 83 replaces build 40. LeaveWell is an account-free, local tenancy checkout record app. No reviewer sign-in or demo credentials are required.

Review flow: finish onboarding, create a property, choose rooms and open Start checkout. The checkout workspace connects a fixture walkthrough, linked evidence, move-in/out comparisons, readings/access scope and final handover review. Each fixture check retains its own observation and evidence links; a photograph alone does not complete a check.

Record fixture observations, link/import evidence, pair an inventory entry with an exit record, resolve meter/access details or provide explicit non-applicability reasons, and save the final handover checkpoint. Editing case details afterwards changes the checkpoint to Review needed. The PDF includes selected observations/comparisons and outstanding details. Excluding either comparison source excludes that comparison observation.

Free features include record capture, typed/audio notes, checklist, meters, keys, manual comparisons, access to saved records and original-file export. LeaveWell Plus Monthly (com.LeaveWell.app.plus.monthly) and Annual (com.LeaveWell.app.plus.annual) provide the same paid feature: unlimited PDF report creation/regeneration while subscribed. StoreKit supplies local prices; no free trial is offered. Open Settings > Explore LeaveWell Plus for purchase, Restore purchases and Manage subscription.

Build 83 reads verified active entitlements before forcing account sync, rechecks verified access after sync success/failure and handles cancellation quietly. Camera, microphone, speech and reminders are optional and requested for the corresponding action. OCR suggestions require confirmation.
