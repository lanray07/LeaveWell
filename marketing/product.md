# LeaveWell: Move Out Checklist

**Move out organised. Leave with the evidence.**

Moving out comes with enough to remember. LeaveWell helps renters in England prepare a calm, organised record before handing back the keys.

## Your rental checkout, one step at a time

Create your property record and dated move-out plan. Work through each room, photograph the details, and keep factual notes with the evidence they describe. Record final meter readings, key quantities, handover dates and recipients.

Keep the original inventory, cleaning receipts and checkout correspondence together. Review move-in documents alongside your current evidence and write your own comparison notes.

## An evidence report you can share

With LeaveWell Plus, choose which evidence to include, review your sharing controls and create a structured PDF with room records, meter readings, keys and supporting documents. Preview it, save it or share it when you are ready. Regenerate reports as your records change throughout your tenancy.

## LeaveWell Plus

LeaveWell Plus Monthly is £2.99 per month in the UK. LeaveWell Plus Annual is £19.99 per year, billed in full annually. Both auto-renewing plans include the same unlimited PDF report features. Local prices are shown in the App Store purchase screen. There is no free trial or minimum annual commitment on the monthly plan.

Photos, notes, document storage, checklists, meter and key records, manual comparisons, access to existing records and export of original files remain free. If your subscription ends, you retain your records and any PDFs you saved. Creating new PDFs requires an active subscription. Plus does not include cloud storage, collaboration or legal advice.

Manage or cancel through your Apple Account's subscription settings. Restore purchases from the LeaveWell Plus screen on any device signed into the purchasing Apple Account. Restoring a subscription restores paid access; local tenancy records do not transfer automatically between devices.

## Private, on your device

No account is needed. Your property records stay on your device. Optional app lock uses Face ID or your device passcode. You choose what to document and share.

Records are excluded from automatic backups. Export anything you need to keep before changing devices or deleting the app.
