# LeaveWell support

## Getting started

Complete the introduction, then create a move-out record for your property. Add your rooms and move-out date. Open a room to capture or import photographs, add a factual note and review the fixtures you have checked.

Your property record also contains your move-out plan, meters, keys, document vault, evidence timeline and PDF report.

## How do I save an evidence report?

Open your property's report screen. Choose the evidence to include and review the contact, deposit and declaration options. Generate and preview the PDF, then use the share sheet to save it to Files or share it with your chosen recipient.

## How do I keep a copy of everything?

In Settings, choose **Export all records and original files**. Save the JSON record and all original files together. The JSON uses an `originals/` folder: place the exported media in that folder when archiving. An exported PDF is a separate report snapshot.

Keep your exports before deleting the app, deleting a property record or replacing your device. Records are excluded from automatic device backups.

## What if camera or microphone access is declined?

You can still type notes and import files. Change permissions in your device's Settings when you want to use capture features. Speech transcription is available only where on-device recognition is supported; saved audio and typed notes remain available.

## How do I delete a property record?

In LeaveWell Settings, choose **Delete record** for the property and confirm. This removes that record and its evidence from the app. Copies you previously exported or shared are kept by their destination or recipient.

## I need help with a problem

[Contact the developer through GitHub Issues](https://github.com/lanray07/LeaveWell/issues/new). Include the LeaveWell version, your iOS version, what you were trying to do and any error message. GitHub Issues are public. Please do not post home addresses, tenancy documents, photos of possessions, financial details or other private evidence.

For advice about a tenancy or deposit dispute, contact an appropriate tenant adviser. LeaveWell helps organise records and does not make legal decisions.
